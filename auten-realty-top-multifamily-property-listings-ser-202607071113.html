Multifamily Property Due Diligence: Best Practices

Thorough due diligence is paramount before acquiring multifamily properties to avoid unforeseen costs and maximize returns. Auten Realty’s brokerage services include comprehensive due diligence support tailored to multifamily investments.

Key Areas of Focus

  • Physical Inspections: Ensuring structural integrity, HVAC systems, roofing, plumbing, and electrical systems are inspected by certified professionals.
  • Financial Review: Analyzing rent rolls, operating expenses, historical income statements, and cap rates against market comps.
  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Verifying compliance with zoning regulations, occupancy permits, and any outstanding litigation or code violations.
  • Tenant Profiles: Reviewing lease agreements, tenant payment history, and evaluating tenant turnover rates to assess income stability.
  • Environmental Assessments: Conducting Phase I Environmental Site Assessments to identify potential contamination or hazards on the property.

Auten Realty collaborates directly with inspectors, accountants, attorneys, and environmental consultants to provide a one-stop due diligence experience, reducing delays and minimizing risk.
