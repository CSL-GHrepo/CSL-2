Common Misconceptions About Real Estate Commissions in Friendswood

Despite growing transparency, several misconceptions about commercial real estate commissions persist, potentially confusing buyers and sellers. Addressing these helps clarify what clients can realistically expect:

  • “Commission is a Fixed Cost”: Commission rates are typically negotiable and may vary by deal rather than being an inflexible percentage.
  • “Higher Commission Means Better Service”: While experience factors in, higher commissions don’t always equate to superior service; assessing broker track record and client testimonials is more telling.
  • “Commission Includes All Costs”: Some fees related to legal counsel, inspections, or financing are separate from brokerage commissions and should be budgeted accordingly.
  • “Buyers Don’t Pay Commission”: In commercial multifamily transactions, commissions are commonly paid by sellers but buyer agency agreements can stipulate fees; it varies by contract.

Educated clients are empowered to ask the right questions and negotiate terms that best fit their unique transaction scenarios.
