Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended pool cleaning frequency in Weston, FL?

Pools in Weston should be cleaned weekly to remove debris, prevent algae growth, and maintain water clarity. This includes skimming the surface, brushing the walls, vacuuming the pool floor, and checking water levels to prevent excessive evaporation or overflow.

How often should pool chemicals be balanced in Weston pools?

Chemical balancing should be performed at least once a week by homeowners and monthly by professional services. Maintaining proper pH, chlorine, alkalinity, and calcium hardness levels is essential due to the intense Florida sun and heavy pool usage common in Weston.

Do commercial pools in Weston require more frequent maintenance than residential pools?

Yes, commercial pool maintenance in Weston typically requires daily monitoring and cleaning due to higher bather loads, increased exposure, and stricter regulatory standards, compared to residential pools which usually need weekly service.

Why is seasonal pool servicing important in Weston, FL?

Seasonal servicing prepares pools for climate-related changes such as heavy summer rains or winter periods of low usage. It includes equipment tuning, deep cleaning, leak checks, and chemical shock treatments to keep pools safe and operational year-round.
