Tax Incentives and Financing Programs for Multifamily Investors in San Antonio

San Antonio’s municipal and state governments offer an array of incentives designed to stimulate multifamily residential construction and renovation —beneficial tools for investors seeking to improve ROI and streamline project feasibility.

Historic Preservation Tax Credits

Notably, properties within certain San Antonio districts may qualify for Texas Historic Preservation Tax Credits. These credits apply to rehabilitation of historic structures, providing up to 25% credit on qualified expenditures. Investors often combine these credits with federal programs to substantially lower project costs.

  • Example: Renovating an older multifamily complex in San Antonio's historic King William neighborhood could unlock these incentives, encouraging preservation while enhancing asset value.

Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) Opportunities

San Antonio also participates in the federal LIHTC program, which incentivizes the development of affordable multifamily housing. This presents an attractive option for investors targeting stable occupancy and long-term government-backed rent subsidies.

  • Benefit: LIHTC projects typically secure non-recourse financing and attract socially conscious capital, diversifying an investor’s portfolio while addressing local housing needs.

Municipal Grant and Assistance Programs

The City of San Antonio offers various grants and financing assistance, especially in targeted revitalization zones. These programs may reduce upfront capital needs or support infrastructure improvements that benefit multifamily properties.

  • Tip: Engaging with local economic development offices early can help investors uncover off-market opportunities and public-private partnership avenues.
