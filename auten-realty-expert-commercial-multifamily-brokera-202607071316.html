Government-Backed Loans and Programs

Programs such as those offered through Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, and the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provide options tailored to multifamily properties:

  • Fannie Mae Multifamily Loans: Favorable for stabilized properties with consistent income streams.
  • Freddie Mac Multifamily Products: Flexible terms for acquisition, refinance, or renovation projects.
  • HUD 221(d)(4) and 223(f): Widely used for new construction or substantial rehabilitation, often paired with affordable housing incentives.

Auten Realty’s expert guidance enables clients to identify the most suitable programs and compile competitive loan applications.
