Financing and Investment Considerations for Multifamily Sellers and Buyers

Multifamily real estate investments are often leveraged with specialized financing products that differ significantly from single-family home loans. Understanding these options helps sellers anticipate buyer needs and position listings advantageously.

Common Financing Options for Multifamily Properties

  • Conventional Commercial Loans: Typically require a down payment of 25% or more, with underwriting based on property cash flow and borrower experience.
  • Government-Backed Financing: Programs like FHA multifamily loans and Fannie Mae’s DUS loan can offer attractive terms, including lower down payments and longer amortization schedules.
  • Bridge Loans: Short-term financing for buyers planning renovations or repositioning before refinancing long-term debt.
  • Private Equity and Investor Partnerships: Syndicated deals and real estate funds are increasingly common among multifamily investors looking to diversify risk and leverage institutional management.
