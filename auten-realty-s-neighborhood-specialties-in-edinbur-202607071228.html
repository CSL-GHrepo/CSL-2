Multifamily property financing in Edinburg often combines traditional mortgage loans with more specialized products, such as:

  • Federal Housing Administration (FHA) Multifamily Loans: Offering low down payment options and attractive interest rates, FHA loans are well-suited for investors looking to acquire or refinance properties with moderate to strong occupancy levels.
