Key Financial Metrics Considered

Understanding the financial health and profitability of a commercial property is foundational. Auten Realty experts assess metrics such as:

  • Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate): This ratio helps investors estimate the property's potential return based on net operating income relative to its current market value.
  • Cash-on-Cash Return: Measures annual pre-tax cash flow against the total cash investment, providing an indicator of immediate yield.
  • Gross Rent Multiplier (GRM): A quick valuation tool comparing the property’s price with its gross rental income to gauge relative value.
  • Net Operating Income (NOI): Essential for understanding the property’s profitability once operating expenses are deducted.
  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): Important for financing considerations, illustrating whether rental income can cover debt obligations.

Auten Realty’s specialists accompany clients through the interpretation of these metrics to ensure informed investment decisions align with risk tolerance and goals.
