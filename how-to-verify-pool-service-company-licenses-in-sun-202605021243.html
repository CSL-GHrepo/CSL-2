Follow these actionable steps to verify a Sunrise pool maintenance company license quickly and confidently:

  1. Identify the Pool Service Provider: Get the full business name, license number, or the technician’s name.
  2. Visit the Florida DBPR Website: Navigate to the License Verification Portal.
  3. Enter Search Details: Input the company or technician name, license number, or location (Sunrise, FL) in the search fields.
  4. Review License Status: Confirm the license is active, not expired, suspended, or revoked.
  5. Verify Specialty Certifications: Check if the pool cleaning or servicing specialty is listed and valid.
